# Release record — v0.23: Never automate inefficiency

**Status:** In progress — not released. The release gate
[#480](https://github.com/jwogrady/spark/issues/480) is open and closes last;
final release approval is human-owned. Release Please PR
[#618](https://github.com/jwogrady/spark/pull/618) is open (awaiting the
human merge that is the release act).

This record is the durable account of v0.23. While it is in progress it states
what has merged and what the gate still needs; it does **not** claim the release
shipped. Per the [release-records convention](README.md), the field evidence is
cited here, not transcribed.

## Thesis

Never automate inefficiency: before scaling autonomous loops, make execution
efficient, observable, and truthful, and keep state-versus-provenance ownership
honest.

## Closing-keyword entries vs. current contract completion

The generated `CHANGELOG.md` is a Release Please projection of the Conventional
Commits on `master`; its `closes #NNN` keywords record the issue a commit closed
**at merge time**, not a claim that the issue's product contract is complete now.
Four such issues landed **foundational or partial** implementation in v0.23 and
were then reopened with **expanded** contracts scoped to v0.24 (release gate
[#481](https://github.com/jwogrady/spark/issues/481)); their broader contracts are
**not** completed v0.23 scope:

- **#484** — the docs-truth check shipped and is a binding release gate in v0.23;
  its expanded release-readiness contract is v0.24.
- **#584** — the independent OpenAI reviewer lane's foundation shipped (it reviews
  v0.23 self-host today); completing that reviewer lane is v0.24.
- **#623** — mutation authority scoped to a resolved repository identity landed;
  the full fail-closed cross-repository-handoff contract is v0.24.
- **#626** — treating project validation commands as mutation-capable landed; the
  complete read-only-assessment detection contract is v0.24.

Immutable commit history is preserved as-is; this note distinguishes what merged
from what the current product contract still owes, per the release-records
convention that the durable release record — not the generated changelog — is the
authority on what a release certified.

## Certification status — gate #480

Open. Correctness evidence is strong (green suites, doctor healthy, honest
one-review-per-HEAD repair cycles in self-host). The gate's **measurement
criterion is not yet satisfied**: there is a single #476/#574 execution baseline
(~35m / ~53.5k tokens, full suite 69/69) and #609 hot-path point measurements, but
no comparable before/after pair, and three of five representative workloads are
`NOT ASSESSED` — so the "material improvement in at least two dimensions" claim is
unproven. The [usage-evidence report](v0.23-usage-evidence.md) records this in
full and leaves that criterion open rather than inferring improvement.
